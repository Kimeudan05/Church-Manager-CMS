Curriculum Creation Guide
Curriculum Types:
  • Sunday School - Weekly lessons for children, youth, or adults
  • Bible Study - In-depth study of books or topics of the Bible
  • Discipleship - Training for spiritual growth and leadership
  • Training - Skills development for ministry roles
  • New Members - Orientation for new church members
  • Leadership - Training for church leaders and volunteers
Status Options:
  • Draft: Only visible to admins, can be edited
  • Active: Visible to target group, lessons can be taught
  • Completed: All lessons taught, read-only mode
  • Archived: Historical record, not visible to members
Planning Tips:
  • Start Date: When the first lesson is scheduled
  • End Date: When the curriculum should be completed
  • Total Lessons: Plan for holidays and breaks
  • Target Group: Match curriculum difficulty to audience
Best Practices:
  • Clearly define learning objectives
  • Match curriculum to group needs and maturity
  • Include both teaching and interactive elements
  • Provide resources for both teachers and students
  • Allow flexibility for Holy Spirit guidance
